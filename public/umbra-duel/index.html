HOW TO FIGHT

Controls

Player 1: move A/D · jump W · crouch S · weapon F · kick G · throw/grab H · spirit R
Player 2: move ←/→ · jump ↑ · crouch ↓ · weapon K/Num1 · kick L/Num2 · throw/grab ;/Num3 · spirit P/Num0
Online: either key set works for you. Esc leaves the fight.

Fighting

Tap weapon three times for your weapon's 3-hit combo. Forward / Back / Down + weapon, and weapon in the air, give four more techniques. Kicks work the same way (Down + kick = leg sweep).
Hold Back (away from your rival) to block high and mid attacks; hold Down + Back to block low attacks. Throw key next to your rival = unblockable hip toss.
Head hits deal 30% more — helmets reduce that. Armor reduces body damage but slows your footwork.
Landing and taking hits fills your Spirit bar. When it is full, press spirit for your weapon's spirit technique.
Weapon perks: Bleed / Burn / Poison deal damage over time, Frost slows, Shock lengthens stun, Drain heals you.
Best of 3 rounds, 60 seconds each.
